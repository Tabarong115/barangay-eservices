Citizens' Charter

Barangay 7, Poblacion, Salcedo, Eastern Samar

In compliance with Republic Act 11032 (Ease of Doing Business and Efficient Government Service Delivery Act of 2018)

Mandate, Vision, Mission & Service Pledge

Mandate

To serve as the primary unit of government that delivers basic services to the community, ensures peace and order, and promotes the general welfare of the residents of Barangay 7, Poblacion, Salcedo, Eastern Samar.

Vision

A progressive, peaceful, and self-reliant community where residents enjoy quality public services, economic opportunities, and a safe environment anchored on good governance and people participation.

Mission

To provide efficient, transparent, and accessible public services; maintain peace and order; promote community development; and ensure the welfare of every resident through responsive governance and citizen engagement.

Service Pledge

We commit to:

  • Deliver services with professionalism, integrity, and courtesy
  • Process requests within prescribed timeframes
  • Maintain transparency in all transactions
  • Provide clear information on requirements and procedures
  • Ensure fair and equal treatment for all residents

Complaints and Feedback

How to File a Complaint

If you experience any issues with our services or wish to file a complaint regarding:

  • Long processing times beyond stated standards
  • Rude or unprofessional behavior by staff
  • Request for unauthorized fees
  • Non-compliance with procedures stated in this Charter

Channels for Complaints:

In Person:

Visit the Barangay Hall and speak with the Punong Barangay or Secretary

Phone:

Contact the Barangay Hotline (if available)

Written:

Submit a written complaint to the Barangay Secretary

Complaint Resolution Process:

  1. Complaint received and recorded
  2. Investigation conducted within 3-5 working days
  3. Resolution communicated to complainant
  4. Corrective actions implemented if needed
